HOW TO PLAY

ABOUT THE GAME

Ride City is a small open-world city ride. Start at Niyal's home, walk to the parking area, choose a bike or car, and explore the streets at your own pace. Traffic moves around the city, and the day/night cycle changes the city lighting.

ON FOOT

  • W / ↑ walk forward; S / ↓ walk back.
  • A and D turn left and right.
  • SHIFT hold to run; SPACE to jump.
  • P to fire while on foot; a pedestrian hit by a shot stays down. ENTER is a close melee hit.

BIKES AND CARS

  • Walk close to a parked or stopped vehicle, then press E or F to get in. Press again to get out.
  • W accelerate, S brake or reverse, and A/D steer.
  • Hold SHIFT for boost. On a bike, SPACE works as a handbrake.
  • Press B to call your bike nearby.

HELICOPTER AND TANK

  • Press H to spawn your helicopter or T to spawn your tank nearby; then walk close and press E or F to enter.
  • In the helicopter, hold W to lift and fly forward, S to descend, and A/D to turn. Hold SHIFT for more flight speed.
  • In the tank, use W/S to drive and A/D to steer. Hold SPACE to fire shells; a shell can flip a car or bus it hits.
  • Press E or F again to exit either vehicle.

CAMERA AND CITY

  • C changes between chase and first-person camera.
  • Drag the screen or mouse to look around; use the mouse wheel to zoom.
  • Use the DAY MODE / NIGHT MODE control to switch lighting. The cycle can also change automatically during a ride.
  • The mini-map shows nearby streets and marks your home with H. ESC pauses the game.

PHONE TOUCH CONTROLS

  • Hold and move the left joystick to walk or steer; drag the right side to turn the camera.
  • Use the on-screen F · CAR, B · BIKE, BOOST, CAM, HIT, and JUMP controls.
TIP · Start on foot, walk beside a vehicle, then press E or F.
